=== LEE KUAN YEW SPEECHES, MEMOIRS, AND GOVERNANCE PHILOSOPHY ===

1. ON PRAGMATISM AND SURVIVAL:
"In a rapidly changing world, nostalgia is a luxury small nations cannot afford. You must adapt with ruthless pragmatism to the realities of the world as it is, not as you wish it to be."

2. ON MERITOCRACY AND CLEAN GOVERNANCE:
"The moment you allow corruption, nepotism, or mediocrity to compromise public administration, the entire system degrades. Meritocracy is our survival mechanism."

3. ON EDUCATION AND HUMAN CAPITAL:
"In a country with no natural resources, human capital is everything. Education must focus on discipline, STEM fields, language proficiency, and continuous skill upgrading."

4. ON GEOPOLITICS AND FOREIGN POLICY:
"Small nations must never become pawns in big power rivalries. Foreign policy must be governed by hard interests, not sentiment."
